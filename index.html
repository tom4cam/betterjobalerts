How it works

  1. 1Send one emailThe role, your city, whether remote is fine, a pay floor and titles to skip. Or attach a resume, or send your LinkedIn link.
  2. 2Reply CONFIRMA short note comes back. Reply with the word CONFIRM and your alerts begin.
  3. 3Get your alertsMonday, Wednesday and Friday, or weekly. New listings are marked, and pay shows when the company posts it.
  4. 4Tell it what fitsTap 👍 or 👎 under a job, with a reason if you like. BJ hides what you pass on and puts what you like first.
  5. 5Prep me and Employer reviewsOne tap gets interview prep for a job, or a sourced rundown of what people say about working there.
  6. 6Change it any timeReply to any alert in plain language, like adding a search or switching to weekly. There's no settings page.

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. Better Job Alerts is free.

Does it apply for jobs for me?

No. It sends you links and listings. You decide what to apply for and apply yourself.

How do I change my alerts?

Reply to any alert and say what you want, in your own words. BJ reads replies every few minutes.

How do I stop?

Reply UNSUBSCRIBE to any alert.
