Better Job Alerts

Privacy Policy · Last updated 11 October 2026

The short version. I run a small, free email service that sends you job openings matching what you asked for. You tell me what you are looking for, by email or by sending a resume or a LinkedIn link, and I send you matching jobs.

I do not sell your personal information. If I ever show ads in these emails, they will be chosen from broad categories only, you will be told before it starts, and you will be able to opt out and keep the alerts.

Who I am

Better Job Alerts is operated by Tom Caswell as an individual, not a company. Contact: betterjobalerts@gmail.com

What I collect

You give me

  • Your email address.
  • What you are looking for: job titles, location, how far you will travel, whether you want remote work, and anything else you tell me in a reply.
  • Optionally, a resume, which may contain your name, phone number, address, work history, education and other details you chose to include.
  • Optionally, a link to your LinkedIn profile.
  • The content of emails you send me.

Collected automatically

  • Whether a message was delivered, and bounce or complaint notices from mail providers.
  • Basic send records: which alerts went to which address and when.
  • Whether an alert was opened, and which links in it were clicked. Emails may include a small tracking image and links that record the click before sending you on. This tells me which searches are worth running and, if advertising is introduced later, how an ad performed. Turning images off in your mail app stops the open measurement.

I do not collect payment details, government identifiers, or your contacts.

Automated processing

I use AI services (currently Anthropic and OpenAI) to read your request, read a resume you send, and turn it into search criteria. Your text is sent to those providers to do that. They act as processors on my behalf and, under their business terms, do not use the content to train their models.

No decision with a legal or similarly significant effect is made about you automatically. Everything here is a suggestion of jobs to look at.

Who else sees it

  • Email delivery: Google (Gmail), which carries the mail.
  • AI processing: Anthropic and OpenAI, as described above.
  • Hosting and storage: Google Cloud and GitHub, where the service runs and where subscriber records are stored.
  • Job sources: public employer job boards. I read them, I do not send them anything about you.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are used under California law. If that ever changes, I will update this policy, tell you by email before it takes effect, and give you a way to opt out first.

Advertising

There are no ads today. If I add them, these rules apply, and I am stating them now so the terms do not change quietly later:

  1. Your resume and your search criteria will not be sent to advertisers. Ads may be chosen using broad categories derived from them, for example “nursing” or “sales”, but the underlying material stays with me.
  2. No third-party ad network gets your email address. If an ad appears, I place it in the email myself. Advertisers receive aggregate counts, not recipient lists.
  3. Ads will be labelled as sponsored or an advertisement.
  4. Opt out and keep the service. You will be able to reply to ask for alerts without ads, and you will still receive the alerts.
  5. Notice first. You will be told by email before the first ad is sent.

If an ad is ever chosen using tracking that follows you across other sites or services, that counts as “sharing” under California law, and I will provide a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” mechanism and honour Global Privacy Control signals before that starts.

How long I keep it

  • Subscriber records and search criteria: while you are subscribed, and up to 12 months after you unsubscribe so a re-subscribe is not a cold start.
  • Resume text: until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it.
  • Message processing records: about 30 days.

Ask me to delete any of it sooner and I will.

Your choices

Everyone. Reply UNSUBSCRIBE to any email to stop immediately. Reply asking me to delete your resume, change your search, or remove your record entirely. Reply asking for a copy of what I hold about you.

California (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing. I do not sell or share as defined there. A resume can contain sensitive personal information; I use it only to build your job search, which is a purpose that does not require a separate limitation right. I will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

EU and UK (GDPR). You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and may complain to your supervisory authority. This is a US-based service, so data is processed in the United States.

To use any of these, reply to one of the emails, or write to betterjobalerts@gmail.com. I will respond within 30 days.

Children

This service is not for anyone under 16, and I do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has subscribed, tell me and I will delete the record.

Security

Credentials are stored in a managed secret store and subscriber records are kept in a private repository. This is a small personal service, not an enterprise system, so please do not send anything you would not want stored in an ordinary email account.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, including anything to do with advertising, you will get an email before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
